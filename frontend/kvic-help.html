Frequently Asked Questions

Open the Honey Batches section from the monitoring menu. You can view batch identification, beekeeper information, production details and quality status.
Select Laboratory Results from the monitoring menu. The page provides test information and quality parameters associated with registered honey batches.
Alerts indicate conditions that may require attention. Different statuses can be used to distinguish normal notifications, warnings and critical issues.
Open the Reports section and select the required report category. Choose the relevant date range or filters before generating the report.
Verify the batch or beekeeper details first. If the information remains incorrect, submit a support request using the contact form below.
