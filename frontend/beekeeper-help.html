Frequently Asked Questions

Open the My Hives section from the sidebar and use the available option to register a new hive. Enter the required hive information and save the record.
Hive monitoring can display information such as temperature, humidity, weight and bee activity. These values help you observe changes in hive conditions.
Open Hive Monitoring and review the latest readings. Check the alert details and inspect the hive if necessary. Persistent or serious issues can be reported through support.
Open the Harvests section and enter the relevant harvest information, including the hive, date and quantity. Save the record after checking the entered information.
Honey batches can be associated with recorded harvest information. Open Honey Batches to create or review batch records and their associated information.
Traceability allows relevant honey information to be followed through different stages, such as hive, harvest, batch processing and distribution.
AI Insights provide data-based observations from available hive information. They can highlight unusual patterns or conditions that may require further attention.
Check that the relevant hive monitoring equipment is operating correctly. Refresh the monitoring page and check again. If the issue continues, contact technical support.
